About Spec Gap

A small, independent channel with one simple question: does the car live up to its spec?

Know the difference

Every car comes with a list of numbers covering range, economy, power and space. Spec Gap exists to find out how those numbers hold up in the real world. We take cars out on real roads, run repeatable tests and report what we find.

Why we started

We grew up on shows that made car testing fun, and we wanted to bring that spirit to the cars normal people actually drive. We keep the tests simple and the results honest.
