How we test

Same routes, same rules, honest results. Here's what goes into each Spec Gap format.

01

Tank Test

We fill the tank to the first click, reset the trip computer and drive a mixed route at legal, realistic speeds until the car runs dry or reaches a safe stopping point. Then we compare the real range and economy with the manufacturer's claim.

02

Head to Head

Two rivals, same day, same roads. We compare performance, comfort, practicality, tech and value, then decide whether the spec gap is worth the price gap.

03

Full Review

A detailed look at one car covering how it drives, what it's like to live with, what it costs to run and who it's for. We give honest verdicts with no hype.

04

Challenges

Top Gear-style challenges with simple rules: cold starts, boot-space tests, budget builds and anything else worth arguing about. Entertaining, but still fair.

Our principles

  • Consistent routes and conditions wherever possible
  • We always state how a test was run
  • Safety and the law come first
  • We tell you when a car isn't loaned or sponsored, and when it is
